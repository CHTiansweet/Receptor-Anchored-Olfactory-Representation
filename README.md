# Receptor-Anchored-Olfactory-Representation
The repository of our paper Receptor-Anchored Olfaction Representation through Perception-Consistent Metric Learning
receptormap_train_ready.ipynb: the notebook of model training and evaluation
model_checkpoint.pth: parameter checkpoint
express_clean.csv: express level of ORs in olfaction mucosa
groundtruth_small: data of direct OR-odorant affinity measurement
cleanedreceptor.csv: human OR sequences and names
datacommon etc: odorants with nature language descriptors for our training
